PHPNuxBill was first made for selling vouchers. You create vouchers in PHPNuxBill and sell them to customers. A voucher never goes into the Mikrotik. Instead, a login template on the router sends customers to PHPNuxBill to register. The customer buys a voucher, registers, and activates it in PHPNuxBill. PHPNuxBill then adds the customer to the Mikrotik, and they sign in on the router's login page with their PHPNuxBill account.

Payment gateways and easy registration changed this. Vouchers are now one option among several: customers can buy a package straight from the payment gateway in PHPNuxBill.
